# Pearlz Home Systems — Refund & Returns Policy
**Document ID:** POL-REF-2026.1  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Return window
Customers may request a return within **14 calendar days** of the delivery date for products purchased from the Pearlz online store. Retail-partner purchases must follow that retailer’s return desk; Pearlz will honour partner returns only when the partner issues an authorised RMA.

## 2. Condition requirements
| Condition | Eligible for full refund? | Who pays return shipping? |
|---|---|---|
| Unopened, original packaging, seals intact | Yes | Pearlz (prepaid label) |
| Opened, unused, all accessories present, original packaging | Yes, minus restocking fee of 8% | Customer |
| Opened and installed / used with water or air flow | No refund; may qualify for replacement if DOA within 7 days | N/A |
| Missing accessories, damaged packaging from customer mishandling | No | Customer (if return attempted) |
| Defective on arrival (DOA) reported within 7 days | Replacement or full refund | Pearlz |

## 3. Non-refundable categories
- Opened filter cartridges and any consumable marked “hygiene-sealed”
- Promotional gift items with zero standalone price
- Downloadable app subscriptions and digital vouchers
- Products damaged by the customer after delivery
- Custom-engraved AeroPearl Mini units

## 4. Partial refunds and restocking
Opened-but-unused returns incur an **8% restocking fee** deducted from the refund. Bundle promotions: if any item in a bundle is missing, the refund is calculated on remaining items at the proportional promotional price, not the standalone list price.

## 5. Refund processing time by payment method
| Payment method | Processing time after warehouse receipt |
|---|---|
| Credit / debit card | 5–7 business days |
| Pearlz Wallet | 1–2 business days |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | Bank transfer within 10 business days after identity verification |
| Partner financing | Per financing partner schedule (Pearlz issues credit note within 5 days) |

## 6. Return vs replacement vs warranty repair
- **Return:** customer sends the product back within the return window for a refund under this policy.
- **Replacement:** DOA or wrong-item shipments; a new unit is shipped; the defective unit is collected.
- **Warranty repair:** after the return window, faults covered by the Warranty Policy are repaired or components replaced under warranty — this is not a refund.

## 7. Promotional bundles
Refunds for promotional bundles are limited to the amount actually paid. Free companion filters included in a bundle are non-refundable as standalone items if the main unit is kept.

## 8. How to start a return
Email support@pearlzhome.example with order number, reason, and photographs of packaging condition. Do not ship products without an RMA number. Unauthorised inbound parcels may be refused.
