## Version-conflict guidance for support
When a customer reports “E-06”, ask for the firmware version shown in the Pearlz app. On firmware **2.3 or earlier**, E-06 means filter due soon. On firmware **2.4+**, E-06 means over-temperature — advise unplug and cool-down, not a filter purchase.
