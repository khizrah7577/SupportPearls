# Pearlz Home Systems — Warranty Policy
**Document ID:** POL-WAR-2026.1  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 15 January 2026  
**Applies to:** AquaPearl 300, AquaPearl 500 Pro, AeroPearl Mini, and discontinued models still under extended warranty

## 1. Purpose
This Warranty Policy describes the limited warranty provided by Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. (“Pearlz”) for eligible products purchased from authorised channels.

## 2. Warranty periods by product line
| Product line | Standard warranty | What the period covers |
|---|---|---|
| AquaPearl 300 | 18 months from invoice date | Body, pump, electronics |
| AquaPearl 500 Pro | 24 months from invoice date | Body, pump, electronics, motorised diverter |
| AeroPearl Mini | 12 months from invoice date | Body, fan motor, electronics |
| AquaPearl 200 (discontinued) | 36-month extended warranty if purchased before 1 June 2024 | Body and pump only |
| AeroPearl Classic (discontinued) | 24-month extended warranty if registered within 30 days of purchase | Body and fan motor only |

**How the period is calculated:** The warranty period starts on the **invoice date**, not the delivery date and not the installation date. Keep your original invoice; it is required for every claim.

## 3. What is covered
### 3.1 Covered components
Pearlz warrants that the following will be free from defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use during the warranty period:
- Outer housing / body
- Booster pump (water purifiers)
- Fan motor (air purifiers)
- Control board and electronics
- Motorised diverter valve (AquaPearl 500 Pro only)

Covered repairs are performed at no charge for parts listed above when the claim is approved.

### 3.2 Exclusions (not covered)
The following are **explicitly excluded** from the standard warranty:
- **Consumables**, including all filter cartridges (sediment, carbon, RO membrane, UV lamp, HEPA, and pre-filters)
- Physical damage from drops, cracks, impact, or liquid ingress into electronics caused by misuse
- Damage from unauthorised repair, opening of sealed compartments, or use of non-Pearlz parts
- Commercial, industrial, or continuous multi-shift use
- Damage caused by water quality outside the inlet specification stated in the product manual (TDS, pressure, temperature)
- Damage from improper voltage, surges without a surge protector, or incorrect socket type
- Cosmetic wear, scratches, and colour fading
- Property damage to cabinets, flooring, or surrounding fixtures caused by leaks — the warranty covers the product itself, not consequential property damage

## 4. Transferability
The warranty is transferable once on resale within the original warranty period, provided the new owner registers the transfer at support@pearlzhome.example within 14 days of resale and supplies the original invoice plus proof of transfer. Extended warranties on discontinued models are **not** transferable.

## 5. Extended warranty
An optional 12-month extended warranty may be purchased within 30 days of the invoice date for AquaPearl 300 and AquaPearl 500 Pro. Extended warranty continues the same coverage and the same exclusions. It does not cover consumables.

## 6. How to claim
To start a warranty claim you must follow the claim procedure described in the **Service & Maintenance Agreement** (document POL-SVC-2026.1), Section “Warranty Claim Procedure”. The warranty policy does not itself list the step-by-step claim workflow; that procedure lives in the Service & Maintenance Agreement by design.

Required documents for any claim:
1. Original purchase invoice showing invoice date and SKU
2. Product serial number (on the rating label)
3. Brief description of the fault and, where relevant, photographs
4. Proof of authorised installation if the fault relates to first-run issues within 30 days

## 7. Unauthorised repair
If the product has been opened or repaired by a technician who is not a Pearlz Authorised Service Partner, the remaining warranty on the repaired subsystem is void. The rest of the unit may remain covered only if Pearlz determines the unauthorised work did not affect other subsystems. In practice, customers in this situation are offered paid out-of-warranty service under the Service & Maintenance Agreement.
