# Pearlz Home Systems — Privacy Policy
**Document ID:** POL-PRIV-2026.1  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Who we are
Pearlz Home Systems (Pvt.) Ltd. (“Pearlz”, “we”) operates the online store, mobile app, and customer support channels. Contact for privacy requests: privacy@pearlzhome.example.

## 2. Personal data collected at purchase
When you buy from us we collect:
- Name, billing and shipping address, phone number, email
- Payment confirmation tokens (we do not store full card numbers; the payment processor does)
- Order history and product serial numbers linked to your account

## 3. Mobile app and water-usage telemetry
If you connect an AquaPearl or AeroPearl unit to the Pearlz app, we collect:
- Device serial number and firmware version
- Filter life estimates and reset events
- Optional water-usage volume estimates (litres per day) when the flow sensor is enabled
- App crash logs and coarse device type (OS version)
We do **not** collect continuous audio, camera frames, or precise GPS from the purifier hardware.

## 4. Retention periods
| Data category | Retention |
|---|---|
| Purchase and invoice records | 7 years (tax/compliance) |
| Support tickets | 3 years after closure |
| App telemetry | 24 months rolling |
| Marketing email list | Until you unsubscribe |
| Privacy request logs | 5 years |

## 5. Third parties we share data with
- Courier partners (name, phone, address for delivery)
- Payment processor (payment tokens, amount, order id)
- Cloud hosting provider for the app backend
- Authorised Service Partners when you open a service claim (serial, fault description, contact phone)
We do not sell personal data.

## 6. Your rights
You may request access, correction, deletion, or export of your personal data by emailing privacy@pearlzhome.example. We respond within 30 days. Deletion may be limited where law requires us to keep invoices.

## 7. Cookies
The online store uses essential cookies for cart and login, and optional analytics cookies. You can reject analytics cookies via the banner. We do not use third-party advertising cookies on the checkout pages.

## 8. App data privacy summary (customer-facing)
App-linked water-usage telemetry is retained for 24 months, shared only with our hosting provider and with a Service Partner when you open a claim, and can be deleted by request to privacy@pearlzhome.example subject to legal retention of purchase records.
