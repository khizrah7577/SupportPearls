# Pearlz Home Systems — Shipping Policy
**Document ID:** POL-SHIP-2026.1  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 15 January 2026

## 1. Delivery region tiers
Pearlz delivers within Pakistan only. International shipping is **not offered** at this time.

| Tier | Examples | Standard delivery window |
|---|---|---|
| Metro | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad / Rawalpindi | 1–3 working days |
| Secondary city | Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta, Hyderabad, Gujranwala | 3–5 working days |
| Remote area | Skardu, Gilgit, Gwadar, Turbat, Chitral, and other listed remote postcodes | 5–7 working days |

## 2. Cut-off times for same-day dispatch
Orders confirmed and paid before **14:00 Pakistan Standard Time** on a working day are dispatched the same day from the Karachi or Lahore fulfilment centre (whichever is closer). Orders after 14:00 dispatch the next working day. Weekends and public holidays are not working days for dispatch.

## 3. Free shipping threshold
Standard shipping is free for order totals of **PKR 15,000** or more before tax. Below that threshold, a flat shipping fee of **PKR 450** applies for metro and secondary cities, and **PKR 850** for remote areas.

## 4. Express and next-day delivery
- **Next-day delivery** is offered only in **Karachi, Lahore, and Islamabad** for orders paid before 14:00, at an additional fee of PKR 1,200.
- Next-day delivery is **not offered** for secondary cities or remote areas, including Skardu.
- The documentation does not describe a purchasable exception for remote-area next-day delivery. Customers seeking exceptions must contact support@pearlzhome.example; agents must not invent a next-day option for remote areas.

## 5. Fragile items
Water and air purifiers are packed in double-wall cartons with foam inserts. Carriers are instructed to handle as fragile. Visible carton damage at delivery must be noted on the delivery sheet before signing.

## 6. Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes **two** delivery attempts. After two failures, the parcel is held at the local depot for five working days. If uncollected, it is returned to Pearlz and a re-shipping fee may apply.

## 7. Tracking
A tracking link is emailed and SMS’d when the parcel ships. Tracking updates may lag by up to 12 hours in remote areas.

## 8. Delivery to Skardu and other remote areas
Skardu is classified as remote-area delivery with a **5–7 working day** window under standard shipping. Express and next-day options do not apply.
