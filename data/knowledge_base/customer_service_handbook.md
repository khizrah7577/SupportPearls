## 1. Support hours and channels
| Channel | Hours (Pakistan Standard Time) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Email support@pearlzhome.example | Mon–Sat 09:00–21:00 | Target first response under 15 minutes when assisted by SupportPearlz drafts |
| WhatsApp business line | Mon–Sat 09:00–18:00 | Text only; no voice notes for policy commitments |
| Web contact form | 24/7 intake | Triaged next working shift |
| Phone | Metro callback only, by appointment | Not a walk-in hotline |

Closed on public holidays listed on the intranet calendar.

## 2. Ticket priority definitions
| Priority | Definition | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Safety (shock, smoke, uncontrolled leak) | 30 minutes |
| P2 | No water / total outage on primary drinking unit | 2 hours |
| P3 | Performance issues, app pairing, filter questions | 6 hours |
| P4 | General FAQ, pricing, shipping ETA | 1 working day |

## 3. Escalation matrix
1. Tier-1 agent drafts from knowledge base.
2. Tier-2 senior agent for multi-policy conflicts (e.g. warranty + unauthorised repair).
3. Service Partner dispatch for on-site diagnosis.
4. Legal/compliance for regulatory or media complaints.

## 4. Tone-of-voice rules
- Clear, calm, specific; prefer documented facts over reassurance fluff.
- Never invent discounts, certifications, or delivery exceptions.
- Never approve refunds beyond documented authority without Tier-2 sign-off.
- Compensation authority limits: Tier-1 up to PKR 1,000 goodwill voucher; Tier-2 up to PKR 5,000; above that requires Operations Manager.

## 5. Complaint handling procedure
Acknowledge → gather order/serial → cite policy → offer documented remedy → confirm next step in writing. Log every “no answer found” query for the monthly content backlog.

## 6. Use of AI drafts
Agents may use SupportPearlz drafts but must verify citations before sending anything involving money, legal commitments, or safety. If confidence is `none` or `partial`, do not auto-send.
