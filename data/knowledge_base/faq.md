# Pearlz Home Systems — Frequently Asked Questions
**Document ID:** FAQ-2026.1  
**Version:** 2026.1  
**Last updated:** 15 January 2026

## Ordering
**Q: How do I place an order?**  
A: Order on pearlzhome.example or through an authorised retail partner. Online orders require a delivery address in Pakistan.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**  
A: Yes, before dispatch. After dispatch, use the return process if eligible.

**Q: Do you price-match other stores?**  
A: Pearlz does not operate a formal price-match programme. Promotional prices on the Pricing Guide are the current online prices.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**  
A: Only before dispatch, by emailing support@pearlzhome.example with your order number.

**Q: Do you sell refurbished units?**  
A: Not at this time through the online store.

## Delivery
**Q: How long does delivery take to Karachi?**  
A: Metro tier: 1–3 working days under standard shipping. Next-day is available in Karachi when selected and paid for before 14:00.

**Q: How long to Skardu?**  
A: Remote-area tier: 5–7 working days. Next-day is not available.

**Q: Will someone call before delivery?**  
A: Couriers typically SMS an ETA; a phone call is not guaranteed.

**Q: What if my parcel arrives damaged?**  
A: Note damage on the delivery sheet, photograph the carton, and contact support within 48 hours.

**Q: Do you ship outside Pakistan?**  
A: No. International shipping is not offered.

## Installation
**Q: Can I install the AquaPearl myself?**  
A: Yes, if you follow the Installation Guide and inlet requirements. Incorrect self-installation that damages the unit can void warranty for the affected parts.

**Q: Do you offer professional installation?**  
A: Yes. Professional installation can be booked for PKR 2,500 in metro areas (see Installation Guide).

**Q: Why must I flush the system after installing filters?**  
A: Flushing clears carbon fines and preservatives. Skipping it commonly causes a strange taste. Details are in the Installation Guide first-run procedure.

**Q: What tools do I need for under-sink install?**  
A: Adjustable wrench, PTFE tape, bucket, towel, and a Phillips screwdriver. Full list in the Installation Guide.

## Filters
**Q: How often do I change the sediment filter?**  
A: Every 3–6 months depending on inlet water quality, or when E-03 triggers on AquaPearl 500 Pro.

**Q: How much is a replacement sediment filter?**  
A: See the Pricing Guide for SKU FLT-SED-01 current promotional and list prices.

**Q: Are third-party filters OK?**  
A: Using non-Pearlz filters voids warranty for damage attributed to those filters.

**Q: My water tastes weird after I changed the filter — is it broken?**  
A: Usually incomplete flushing. Run the first-run flush; discard the first two tanks of water.

**Q: Does the filter remove bacteria?**  
A: AquaPearl 500 Pro with UV stage addresses microbiological risk as described in the product manual performance section. Always follow boil-water advisories from local authorities when issued.

## Warranty
**Q: How long is the warranty on the AquaPearl 500 Pro?**  
A: 24 months from the invoice date. See Warranty Policy.

**Q: Are filters covered under warranty?**  
A: No. Filters are consumables and are excluded.

**Q: I had a local technician open my unit — am I still covered?**  
A: Unauthorised repair can void coverage for the affected subsystem. See Warranty Policy §7 and ask about paid service options.

**Q: Does warranty cover a leak that damaged my cabinet?**  
A: The warranty covers the product, not consequential property damage to cabinets or flooring. See Warranty Policy exclusions.

**Q: Can warranty transfer when I sell the unit?**  
A: Once, within the original period, with registration. Extended warranties on discontinued models do not transfer.

## App and account
**Q: The app will not pair.**  
A: Enable Bluetooth, put the unit in pairing mode (hold PAIR 3 seconds), and reboot the phone. See Troubleshooting Guide.

**Q: What data does the app collect?**  
A: See the Privacy Policy for personal data and water-usage telemetry details.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**  
A: Yes. Email privacy@pearlzhome.example to exercise deletion rights under the Privacy Policy.

**Q: Does the app work offline?**  
A: Basic filter timers sync when online; live TDS requires connectivity on AquaPearl 500 Pro.
